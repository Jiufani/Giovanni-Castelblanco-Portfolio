03 / HOW I THINK

What if it
felt better?

My approach connects the why, the how, and the details that make an experience feel right.

✳
01Understand the problem+

Start with the people, the context, and the goal. What feels difficult? What should the experience help someone do?

02Explore the possibilities+

Sketch, compare, and question the obvious answer. Translate the strongest direction into an interface with a clear hierarchy and intentional details.

03Bring it to life+

Build an experience that behaves as well as it looks. Think about responsive layouts, accessibility, and the connection between the interface and its data.

04Learn and refine+

Try it, ask for feedback, and look at the rough edges. The next iteration should make something clearer, easier, or more useful.
